## Important evaluation rules

- Q1: Apply the current Tamil Nadu income threshold and the applicable Section 12 eligibility categories. Do not hardcode outdated income limits.
- Q4: Women are independently eligible under Section 12(c), regardless of income. Explain where to apply and whether the dispute is suitable for Lok Adalat.
- Q5: Explain the difference between a regular Lok Adalat and a Permanent Lok Adalat, including their jurisdiction and decision-making powers.
- Q6: Do not invent or predict Lok Adalat dates. If the information is unavailable in the provided documents, reply exactly: "I don't know."
- Q7: Identify relevant legal aid and worker-related assistance without assuming that every wage dispute is automatically covered by a particular scheme.
- Q8: Explain the availability of free legal aid and relevant victim compensation provisions, distinguishing eligibility and procedures.
- Q9: Explain the eligibility of persons in custody under Section 12(g).
- Q10: Explain SC eligibility under Section 12(c) and the suitability of Lok Adalat for the land dispute.
